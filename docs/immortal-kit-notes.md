# Immortal Kit Notes

This reference preserves player-supplied in-game skill text used when reviewing the v1.5 experimental META. Values below are transcribed from the supplied screenshots.

## Colony Tar

### Core kit

- **Transmutation (Passive):** On Immortal summon, for every 10% ATK increase Tar has, there is a 30% chance to increase one random stat (ATK, ATK SPD, or Range). Max stacks: 10.
- **Fear Sense (Passive):** ATK increases by 1000%. Deals increased damage based on the target's missing HP. Damage increases by 0.5%.
- **Sweep (Skill):** Every 15 attacks, deals 19,000% Physical DMG to enemies in the area. Damage increases by 25% if the target is under the effect of Contaminant.
- **Contaminant (Skill):** 10% chance to attach Contaminant to enemies in the area. Contaminant lasts for 7s, dealing 300% Physical DMG every 0.5s and reducing DEF by 15.
- **Abyss (Ultimate):** Deals 35,000% Physical DMG to enemies in the area. Removes Contaminant from targets and deals 6,500% Physical DMG for each Contaminant removed.

### Level upgrades

- **Lv.6:** Transmutation chance +20%.
- **Lv.9:** ATK SPD +10%.
- **Lv.12:** Contaminant projectile count +1.
- **Lv.15:** ATK and ATK SPD +10%.
- **Lv.16:** Contaminant skill duration +2s.
- **Lv.19:** Transmutation final stack +2.
- **Lv.22:** Abyss damage per Contaminant +1,500%.
- **Lv.25:** Fear Sense skill damage +0.2%.

### Review implications

- Tar is physical and area-damage oriented, with attack count driving Sweep and Contaminant opportunities.
- Contaminant stacks/uptime and Abyss consumption are the key interactions to validate for the final PvP and Guild pair.

## Knight Lancelot

### Level upgrades

- **Lv.3:** ATK +10%.
- **Lv.6:** Skill DMG +10% when the target has Max HP DMG Resistance.
- **Lv.9:** ATK SPD +10%.
- **Lv.12:** Sword of Judgment burn interval -0.5s.
- **Lv.15:** ATK and ATK SPD +10%.
- **LB Lv.16:** Ember Luck Stone cost -5.
- **LB Lv.19:** Lancelots required for Immortal -1.
- **LB Lv.22:** Ember Lv.3 effect +20%.
- **LB Lv.23:** Sword of Judgment leaves a Flame Pillar for 2.5s, dealing 3,000% Magic DMG to enemies in the area every 0.5s.
- **LB Lv.24:** The sword remains for 7s and increases Magic DMG dealt by allies within range by 15%.
- **LB Lv.25:** Flame Pillar DMG +3,500% (6,500% per tick if the bonus is additive, pending an in-game coefficient check).

### Review implications

- LB16 and LB19 improve summon economy and consistency, not direct combat DPS.
- LB22 is potentially meaningful, but its exact team-DPS value depends on the undocumented base Ember Lv.3 effect.
- At five 0.5-second ticks per 2.5-second pillar, LB23 adds 15,000% ATK-scaled Magic-DMG coefficient per Sword of Judgment cast; LB25 raises that estimate to 32,500% if its +3,500% is additive.
- LB24 can add up to 15% Magic DMG to nearby allies while the sword persists. Actual team gain depends on cast frequency, positioning, eligible allied Magic-DMG share, and whether sword durations overlap.
- These milestones strengthen Mastery + Magic in PvE/PvP and Mastery + Hunter in Guild; they do not currently justify a different high-level BiS pair.
